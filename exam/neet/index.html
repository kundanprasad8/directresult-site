NEET Results

NEET UG is India's single entrance exam for MBBS, BDS, AYUSH and BSc Nursing seats, run by the National Testing Agency (NTA). Over 20 lakh candidates appear each year. The result comes as a scorecard showing your marks out of 720, percentile, All India Rank and category rank, followed by the qualifying cut-off and MCC counselling schedule.

How to Check the NEET Results

  1. Go to neet.nta.nic.in and click the NEET UG result/scorecard link.
  2. Log in with your application number, date of birth and the security pin.
  3. Your scorecard opens — check marks, percentile, All India Rank and qualifying status.
  4. Download the scorecard PDF; you will need it for counselling registration.
  5. Note the cut-off for your category, then register for MCC or state counselling before the deadline.

Selection Stages

NEET has one written exam. After it: result and scorecard release, cut-off announcement, then counselling in rounds (MCC for 15% All India Quota and deemed universities, state authorities for 85% state quota). A separate mop-up/stray round fills vacant seats.

What to Do Next

After the result, register for counselling on mcc.nic.in (All India Quota) or your state's counselling portal. Keep your scorecard, admit card, Class 10 and 12 marksheets and category certificate ready.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the official NEET Results result declared?

On National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (UG)'s official portal (neet.nta.nic.in). Check only the official site — forwarded links and social-media posts are often outdated or fake.

What are the stages of the NEET Results selection?

NEET has one written exam. After it: result and scorecard release, cut-off announcement, then counselling in rounds (MCC for 15% All India Quota and deemed universities, state authorities for 85% state quota). A separate mop-up/stray round fills vacant seats.

I lost my registration number. How do I check my result?

Most portals let you recover it with your registered mobile number and date of birth. For PDF-only results, search your name — but confirm with your roll number later, since names repeat.

What is the cut-off and where do I find it?

The cut-off is the minimum marks needed to qualify for the next stage, released category-wise with (or shortly after) each result. It is published as a separate notice or inside the result PDF.

How long after the exam is the NEET Results result declared?

It varies by body and stage — typically 1 to 4 months for written exams. The commission usually gives no advance date; the result simply appears on the portal one day.

What should I do after qualifying?

After the result, register for counselling on mcc.nic.in (All India Quota) or your state's counselling portal. Keep your scorecard, admit card, Class 10 and 12 marksheets and category certificate ready.
