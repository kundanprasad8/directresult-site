KBPE / DHSE Plus Two (Class 12) Result

The Kerala Board of Public Examinations / DHSE (KBPE / DHSE) conducts the Plus Two (Class 12) examinations for schools affiliated in Kerala. Lakhs of students appear every year, and the result is one of the most searched education updates in the state. This page tracks the KBPE / DHSE Plus Two (Class 12) result — expected date, official websites, step-by-step checking process, and what to do after the result.

Result date tracker: the board has not announced this year's exact date yet. Based on previous years, the KBPE / DHSE Plus Two (Class 12) result is expected in May. The official date is announced on keralaresults.nic.in a few days before declaration.

How to Check the KBPE / DHSE Plus Two (Class 12) Result

  1. Go to the official result portal: keralaresults.nic.in (type it directly; do not trust forwarded links).
  2. Find the link titled "KBPE / DHSE Plus Two (Class 12) Result" on the homepage.
  3. Enter your roll number and date of birth exactly as on your admit card.
  4. Your subject-wise marks and overall result appear on screen — download or print the page.
  5. Collect the original marksheet from your school when it arrives; the online copy is provisional.

Other Ways to Get Your Result

  • DigiLocker: digital marksheets appear in the DigiLocker app under Education → your board. Valid for admissions and job applications.
  • SMS: the board notifies an SMS format (for example, a keyword sent to a short code) on result day for areas with weak internet.
  • School notice board: schools receive the full result list and display it; your class teacher can confirm your marks.

Details on Your Plus Two (Class 12) Marksheet

Check these carefully when the result opens: your name spelling, roll number, date of birth, subject-wise theory and practical marks, total, percentage/CGPA, division, and the qualifying status (pass / compartment / fail). Report any error to your school within the correction window.

After the Result: Revaluation and Compartment

If your marks are lower than expected, apply for rechecking/revaluation within the board's window (usually 7–15 days, per-subject fee applies). If you did not clear one or two subjects, compartment exams are held a few weeks later — your school will guide the application. Students who clear all subjects can move to Class 11 admissions (for Plus Two (Class 12) Class 10) or college and entrance-exam applications (for Plus Two (Class 12) Class 12) right away using the provisional marksheet.

Frequently Asked Questions

When will the KBPE / DHSE Plus Two (Class 12) result be declared?

Based on previous years, the Kerala Board of Public Examinations / DHSE usually declares the Plus Two (Class 12) result in May. The board announces the exact date and time a few days in advance through a press note on keralaresults.nic.in. This page is updated as soon as the date is announced.

Where can I check the KBPE / DHSE Plus Two (Class 12) result?

On the board's official website (keralaresults.nic.in), through the DigiLocker app, and via SMS on the number notified by the board. Avoid unofficial sites that ask for money or personal details.

What do I need to check my Plus Two (Class 12) result?

Your roll number and usually your date of birth or school code, exactly as printed on your admit card. Keep the admit card ready before result day.

Is the online Plus Two (Class 12) marksheet valid for admission?

The online result is provisional. Colleges accept it for provisional admission, but you must submit the original marksheet and passing certificate issued by your school once available.

What if I fail in one or two subjects in KBPE / DHSE Plus Two (Class 12)?

Most boards, including KBPE / DHSE, hold compartment (supplementary) exams a few weeks after the main result. Check the compartment date sheet on the board website and apply through your school before the deadline.

How do I apply for revaluation of my Plus Two (Class 12) answer sheets?

The board opens a short rechecking/revaluation window after the result — usually 7 to 15 days. Apply online on keralaresults.nic.in, pay the per-subject fee, and track the revised marks on the same portal.

My name or date of birth is wrong on the Plus Two (Class 12) marksheet. What now?

Contact your school office immediately. Name and date-of-birth corrections go through the school to the board, and there is a fixed correction window — do not delay.

How can I download my Plus Two (Class 12) marksheet from DigiLocker?

Install the DigiLocker app, sign in with your Aadhaar-linked mobile number, go to the Education section, select your board and class, and enter your roll number. The digital marksheet is valid for most official purposes.
