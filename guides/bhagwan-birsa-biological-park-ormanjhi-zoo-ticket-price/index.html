  • Weekly Off: Monday Closed (Remains closed every Monday for animal rest and park maintenance).
  • Best Visiting Months: October to March (Comfortable walking weather).

Location & Connectivity

  • Location: Ormanjhi, NH-33 (Ranchi-Hazaribagh Highway).
  • Distance from Main Ranchi City: Approx. 20 – 22 km.
  • Transport Options: Auto-rickshaws, shared taxis, and local private buses available from Kantatoli Chowk and Main Road.

Key Attractions & Safari Highlights

  • Big Cat Enclosures: Bengal Tigers, Asiatic Lions, and Leopards in naturalistic open habitats.
  • Freshwater Aquarium: One of the largest public aquariums in Eastern India with exotic fish species.
  • Herbivore Safari & Aviary: Spotted deer, sambar, nilgai, and a dedicated bird enclosure.
  • Boating & Children’s Park: Pedal boating facilities and dedicated play areas for kids.
Important Visitor Guidelines: Plastic bags and feeding animals are strictly prohibited. Wear comfortable walking shoes as exploring the entire 104-hectare park on foot takes about 2 to 3 hours.

Key Attractions inside Bhagwan Birsa Biological Park

  • Royal Bengal Tigers & White Tigers: Zoo ka sabse bada attraction yahan ke vishal enclosure mein rehne wale baagh hai.
  • Asiatic Lions & Bears: Lions, Black Bears, aur Hyenas ke enclosures tourists dwara kafi pasand kiye jaate hai.
  • Elephant Enclosure & Deer Safari Area: Sambar, Spotted Deer, aur Barking Deer ke liye alag se bada grassy zone hai.
  • State-of-the-Art Butterfly Park: Saikdon prajatiyon ki colorful butterflies aur herbal plants wala attractive park.
  • Freshwater Fish Aquarium: Alag-alag prajati ki exotic aur local machliyon ka interactive glass display.

How to Reach Ormanjhi Zoo from Ranchi

Ormanjhi Zoo Ranchi shehar ke center se lagbhag 20 km ki doori par NH-33 (Ranchi-Ramgarh Highway) par located hai:

  • By City Bus / Auto: Kantatoli Bus Stand ya Overbridge se Ramgarh/Hazaribagh jane wali local buses aur shared autos direct Ormanjhi Zoo Gate par utarti hai.
  • By Private Car / Bike: Ranchi Main Road se Lalpur, Booty More, aur Neuri Vikas Vidyalaya hote hue Highway 33 par 25-30 minute mein pahuncha ja sakta hai.
  • From Ranchi Railway Station: Distance approx 22 km. Direct taxi ya auto hire ki ja sakti hai.
  • From Birsa Munda Airport: Distance approx 26 km. Airport se NH-33 bypass ring road lekar bina city traffic ke aaram se pahuncha ja sakta hai.

Guidelines & Important Rules for Visitors

  • Plastics & Polythene Banned: Zoo ke andar single-use plastic aur polythene le jana mana hai.
  • Animals ko Khana Dena Sakht Mana Hai: Janwaron ko kisi bhi tarah ka khane-peene ka saman na fekein.
  • Noise & Music System Prohibited: Zoo ke andar loud music bajana ya shor machana strict prohibitted hai taaki animals disturb na hon.
  • No Smoking & Alcohol: Premises ke andar smoking aur alcohol consumption bilkul prohibited hai.
  • Follow Safety Barriers: Enclosure ke safety fences par charhna ya animakls ko chhedna punishable offence hai.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Bhagwan Birsa Biological Park (Ormanjhi Zoo) ka adult ticket price kitna hai?

12 saal ya usse adhik umar ke visitors ke liye Ormanjhi Zoo ka main entry ticket price ₹60 per person hai.

2. Kya Ormanjhi Zoo Monday ko khula rehta hai?

Nahi, Ormanjhi Zoo har Monday (Somwar) ko poori tarah band rehta hai. Somwar ko chhodkar baki sabhi din zoo khula rehta hai.

3. Ormanjhi Zoo ke Aquarium aur Butterfly Park ka combo ticket price kya hai?

Aquarium aur Butterfly Park dono ke combo ticket ka rate Adults ke liye ₹55 aur Children (3-12 Yrs) ke liye ₹25 hai.

4. Kya Ormanjhi Zoo mein bacchon ke liye free entry hai?

3 saal se kam umar ke bacchon ke liye daily free entry rehti hai. Iske alawa 15th August, 26th January, aur Wildlife Week ke dauran 12 saal tak ke sabhi bacchon ke liye entry bilkul free rehti hai.

5. Kya zoo ke andar ghoomne ke liye gadi (vehicle) milti hai?

Haan, zoo ke andar ghoomne ke liye battery-operated electric vehicles (golf carts) available hoti hain, jinka charge ₹240/hour ke aaspas rehta hai.

6. Ormanjhi Zoo Ranchi se kitni doori par hai?

Ormanjhi Zoo Ranchi city center se lagbhag 20 km ki doori par NH-33 Ramgarh highway par sthit hai.
