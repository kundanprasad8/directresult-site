Ranchi University Semester 2 Exam Form Fill Up Date 2026: Janein Kab Se Bhara Jayega Form Aur Kya Hain Naye Rules?

Published: 2026-05-24

Ranchi University (RU) ke alag-alag colleges (jaise Ranchi College, Marwari College, Doranda College, Gossner College, etc.) mein padhai kar rahe Four-Year Undergraduate Programme (FYUGP) aur regular BA, BSc, BCom ke bacho ke liye ek behad zaroori update samne aa raha hai. Agar aap Semester 1 clear karne ke baad apne agle padav par hain, toh aapko bata dein ki Ranchi University Semester 2 exam form fill up date ko lekar examination department jald hi official notification jari karne wala hai.

University ke naye rules ke according, bina exam form bhare aur bina apna admit card generate kiye aapko Semester 2 ki internal ya external exam mein baithne ki permission nahi di jayegi. Is article mein hum aapko batayenge ki Semester 2 ka online form kab se khulega, iski expected fees kya hogi, aur aap bina kisi galti ke apna form kaise bhar sakte hain.

Ranchi University Sem 2 Exam Form 2026 Overview

Form bharne se pehle is session se judi main details par ek nazar daal lete hain:

Expected Dates: Kab Se Bhara Jayega Form?

Ranchi University ke exam board ke mutabik, Semester 2 ke exam forms generally tab shuru kiye jaate hain jab internal assessments (mid-semester exams) almost poore ho chuke hote hain.

  • Online Form Fill-up Start Date (Expected): June 2026 ke pehle ya dusre hafte se link active hone ki poori sambhavna hai.
  • Last Date Without Fine: Notification jari hone ke baad candidates ko bina late fine ke form bharne ke liye lagbhag 7 se 10 din ka samay diya jata hai.
  • With Late Fine Date: Agar aap tay samay par form nahi bhar paate hain, toh ₹400 se ₹500 ke late fine ke sath aapko extra 3-4 din ka mauka aur diya jata hai.

Step-by-Step Guide: Online Exam Form Kaise Bharein?

Ranchi University ka pariksha form ab poori tarah se digital ho chuka hai. Link active hone par aap neeche diye gaye steps ko follow karke apna form khud bhar sakte hain:

1.Ranchi University Exam Domain Par Jayein:

Sabse pehle Ranchi University ke dedicated examination portal ranchiuniversity.ac.in par jayein ya direct unke exam server url par click karein.

2.Examination Form Tab Open Karein:

Homepage par dikh rahe “Examination Form” ya “Online Exam Form” ke link par click karein. Wahan “UG Semester-II (Session 2025-2029) Form” select karein.

3.Login Credentials Aur Details Fill Karein:

Apna University Roll Number / Registration Number aur password daal kar login karein. Agar aap naye hain, toh pehle register karein. Aapki saari basic details aur subjects (Major, Minor, MDC, SEC, VAC) screen par aa jayenge, unhe verify karein.

4.Online Examination Fee Jama Karein:

Sabhi details check karne ke baad “Proceed to Payment” par click karein. Net Banking, Debit Card, ya UPI ke madhyam se apni exam fee (Aamtaur par ₹300 se ₹650 tak, course ke mutabik) jama karein.

5.Confirmation Receipt Print Karein:

Payment success hone ke baad apna Exam Form Aur Fee Receipt PDF download kar lein. Iska printout nikal kar apne college ke counter par verification ke liye zaroor jama karein.

Required Documents Checklist

Form bharne ke time aur use college mein submit karte waqt aapko neeche diye gaye documents ki zaroorat padegi:

  • Semester 1 ka Admit Card aur Marksheet/Result copy.
  • Ranchi University ka Registration Slip.
  • Aadhaar Card aur valid ABC ID Card copy.
  • Passport-size photo aur signature (agar portal par dobara upload karne ko kaha jaye).

FAQs: Aksar Puche Jane Wale Sawal

1. Agar Semester 1 mein mera kisi paper mein ‘Promoted’ lag gaya hai, toh kya main Sem 2 ka form bhar sakta hoon?

Haan, agar aap Semester 1 mein kisi paper mein fail ya promoted hain, toh bhi aap Semester 2 ka exam form bharne ke liye eligible hain. Aapko ‘Regular’ candidate ke roop mein hi form banna hoga.

2. Ranchi University Semester 2 ki exam fee kitni hoti hai?

General aur OBC category ke bacho ke liye regular BA, BSc, BCom ki fee lagbhag ₹600 se ₹650 ke beech hoti hai. SC/ST candidates ke liye isme thodi chhoot di jaati hai.

3. Payment katne ke baad bhi agar portal par ‘Unpaid’ dikhaye toh kya karein?

Agar aapke account se paise kat gaye hain aur form submit nahi hua hai, toh turant dobara payment na karein. Kam se kam 24 se 48 ghante ka intezar karein, University ka server automatic payment ko update kar deta hai. Agar phir bhi na ho, toh college ke computer cell ya university helpdesk se contact karein.
