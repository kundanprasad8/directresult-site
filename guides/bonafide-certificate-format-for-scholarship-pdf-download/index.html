Bonafide Certificate Kya Hota Hai?

Bonafide certificate ek official proof hai jo yeh sabit karta hai ki aap us particular college ya school ke asli (genuine) student hain.

  • Ye sirf aapke college ya school ke Principal/Head master dwara hi jaari (issue) kiya jata hai.
  • Scholarship ka paisa dene se pehle sarkar is paper se aapka roll number aur fees check karti hai.

College Se Bonafide Certificate Kaise Banwayein? (Process)

College se paper banwane ka process ekdum simple hai. Aapko ye 3 steps lene honge:

  1. Apne college ke admin office ya clerk ke paas jayein.
  2. Unhe apna Admission Receipt aur ID card ki photocopy dein aur bole ki e-Kalyan scholarship ke liye Bonafide chahiye.
  3. Agar unke paas format nahi hai, toh upar diye gaye PDF ko letterhead par print karwa kar unse sign aur mohar lagwa lein.

e-Kalyan Scholarship Ke Liye Sakht Niyam (Rules)

Jharkhand e-Kalyan form bharte waqt students aksar kuch galtiyan karte hain jisse unka paisa ruk jata hai. Ye baatein yaad rakhein:

  • Scan Quality: Bonafide certificate hamesha original scan karke upload karein. Mobile se khinchi hui dhundhli photo dalne par DWO reject kar dega.
  • Date Mismatch: Certificate par date isi saal ki (current academic year) honi chahiye. Purane saal ka bonafide lagane par form aage nahi badhega.
  • Overwriting: Agar pen se fee structure likha hai, toh uspar koi kaat-chhaant (overwriting) ya whitener nahi laga hona chahiye.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. Bonafide certificate format kahan se download karein?

Ans: Aap is article ke upar diye gaye PDF link par click karke direct e-Kalyan accepted bonafide format download kar sakte hain aur use print kara sakte hain.

Q2. Kya bonafide certificate ke bina scholarship form bhar sakte hain?

Ans: Bilkul nahi. Bina bonafide certificate aur fee structure ke portal aapka form aage submit hi nahi hone dega. Ye sabse zaroori paper hai.

Q3. College wale bonafide banane ke liye paise mang rahe hain, kya karein?

Ans: Sarkari niyam ke hisaab se bonafide certificate bilkul free banta hai. Agar koi extra fees mangta hai, toh aap apne college ke principal se iski direct shikayat kar sakte hain.

Q4. Mere bonafide par fee structure nahi likha hai, kya form pass hoga?

Ans: Nahi. e-Kalyan officer fee structure dekh kar hi scholarship ka paisa taiyar karte hain. Bina fee detail wale certificate ko DA ya DWO seedha reject kar dete hain.

Q5. Kya pichle saal ka bonafide is saal chal jayega?

Ans: Nahi chalega. Har naye academic year (jaise 2025-26) ke liye aapko college se naya aur current date wala bonafide certificate banwana padta hai.
