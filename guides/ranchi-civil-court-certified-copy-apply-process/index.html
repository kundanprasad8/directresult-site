Ranchi Civil Court Certified Copy Apply Process 2026: Step-by-Step Online & Offline Guide

🏛️ Offline Application Procedure (Ranchi Civil Court Complex)

  1. Form Procurement: Obtain the Certified Copy Application Form (Folio Form) from the stamp vendor or advocate clerk block inside Ranchi Civil Court premises (Near Near Collectorate / Kutchery Chowk).
  2. Fill Case Details: Mention Case Type (e.g., Original Suit, Criminal Misc, Title Suit), CNR Number, Case Number, Year, Court Name (e.g., Principal District & Sessions Judge / AJR Court), and specific document needed (Judgment, Order Sheet, Deposition).
  3. Affix Stamps: Attach required Court Fee Stamps and Folio sheets as per page estimate on the application.
  4. Submit at Copy Section: Hand over the form at the Copying Department (Certified Copy Counter). Note down the Application Receipt Number.
  5. Collection: Return on the designated date, present your receipt, and collect the certified copy bearing official seal and signature.

💻 Online Case Status & e-Courts Certified Copy Tracking

While physical certified copies with official court stamps require counter processing at Ranchi Civil Court, you can get order sheets and track certified copy status digitally:

  • e-Courts Services Portal: Visit ecourts.gov.in or download the eCourts Services App.
  • Search by CNR: Enter the 16-character CNR Number of your Ranchi Civil Court case to view uncertified PDF orders.
  • Jharkhand High Court / District Court Portal: Check the official website (ranchi.dcourts.gov.in) for cause lists, daily orders, and certified copy application status counters.

📄 Essential Particulars Required on Form

  • CNR Number: 16-digit unique case identifier (Available on eCourts).
  • Case Identification: Case Category, Case Number, and Registration Year.
  • Name of Court: Exact Presiding Officer / Judge designation in Ranchi District Court.
  • Party Names: Name of Petitioner / Plaintiff vs Respondent / Defendant.
  • Document Required: Specific details (e.g., “Final Judgment dated DD/MM/YYYY” or “Complete Order Sheets”).
  • Applicant ID: Identity proof and Vakalatnama reference (if filed through advocate).

Step 1: Application Form & Folio Purchase

Civil Court campus ke paas located Stamp Vendors ya Advocate Clerks se Certified Copy Application Form aur zaroori Folio Papers kharidein.

Step 2: Fill Application Form Correctly

Form par case details saaf-saaf fill karein:

  1. Court ka naam (In the Court of…)
  2. Case Number aur Year
  3. Party Name (Vs)
  4. Specific requirement (e.g., “Certified Copy of Final Judgment Dated DD/MM/YYYY”)
  5. Urgent ya Ordinary category mark karein.

Step 3: Attach Court Fee Stamps & Folio

  • Base Court Fee Stamp: Application form par required Value ka Judicial Stamp paste karein.
  • Folio Attachment: Order ke estimated length ke acording se folio sheets attach karein.

Step 4: Verification & Advocate Signature

Form par Case ke Advocate ka signature aur Enrollment Number/Seal lagwayein. Agar party self-apply kar rahi hai, toh identity proof (Aadhaar Card) attach karna zaroori hai.

Step 5: Submission at Copying Counter

Application form ko Ranchi Civil Court Copying Section Counter par submit karein. Counter clerk aapke application ko register karke ek Application Number / Receipt Slip dega.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Kya Ranchi Civil Court me certified copy online apply kar sakte hai?

Filhal online e-Copying portal testing phase me hai. Sabse fast aur reliable tareeka Civil Court Ranchi ke Copying Counter par offline form submit karna hi hai.

2. Folio paper kya hota hai aur ye kahan milta hai?

Folio paper ek special yellow/green court paper hota hai jis par certified copy print ya type hoti hai. Ye Ranchi Civil Court premises ke stamp vendors ke paas easily mil jata hai.

3. Agar case old (purana) ho toh certified copy milne me kitna time lagta hai?

Decided ya old cases ke records Record Room (Mahafezkhan) se fetch kiye jaate hain, isliye purane cases ki copy milne me 15 se 20 din ka time lag sakta hai.

4. Kya bina advocate ke party khud certified copy apply kar sakti hai?

Haan, case ki party (Petitioner ya Respondent) apna valid ID proof (Aadhaar Card) de kar directly Copying Counter par apply kar sakti hai.
