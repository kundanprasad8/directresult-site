Blood Requisition Ke Liye Zaroori Documents

Kisi bhi licensed blood bank se blood ya blood components lene ke liye ye documents sath le jana na bhoolein:

  1. Requisition Form: Doctor ya hospital ka signed aur stamped blood requisition form.
  2. Patient Ka Blood Sample: Cross-matching ke liye properly labeled pilot tube sample.
  3. Replacement Donor: Ek healthy voluntary donor agar blood bank ki policy mei zaroori ho.
  4. Valid ID Proof: Patient ke attendant ka government photo ID.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

General & Operational Questions

1. Ranchi mei online live blood stock availability kaise check karein?

Aap Govt of India ke official portal eraktkosh.mohfw.gov.in ya e-RaktKosh mobile app par real-time inventory check kar sakte hain. Bas State (Jharkhand), District (Ranchi), aur zaroori blood group select karein.

2. Kya blood bank bina replacement donor ke blood dene se mana kar sakta hai?

Nahi. National Blood Transfusion Council (NBTC) guidelines ke mutabiq, koi bhi blood bank emergency cases mei replacement donor ke liye zordarabaar nahi kar sakta. Agar koi refuse kare, toh written reason maangein.

3. Ranchi mei kaun-se blood banks 24 ghante khule rehte hain?

RIMS, Red Cross Society, aur Nagarmal Modi Seva Sadan samet sabhi major blood banks emergency services ke liye 24/7 open rehte hain.

Eligibility & Donation Rules

4. Blood donate karne ke liye kya eligibility honi chahiye?

  • Age: 18 se 65 saal.
  • Weight: Minimum 45 kg (Whole Blood ke liye) aur 50 kg (Platelet/Apheresis ke liye).
  • Hemoglobin: Minimum 12.5 g/dL.
  • Interval: Males har 3 mahine aur Females har 4 mahine mei donate kar sakte hain.

5. Blood units ke liye kya government processing fees hoti hai?

India mei blood bechna illegal hai; sirf testing aur screening ke charges liye jaate hain:

  • Government Hospitals (RIMS/Sadar): Standard processing fee hoti hai (BPL cardholders, Thalassemia, aur Hemophilia patients ke liye bilkul free).
  • Private Centers: NBTC dwara fixed standard capped rates par charges liye jaate hain.
