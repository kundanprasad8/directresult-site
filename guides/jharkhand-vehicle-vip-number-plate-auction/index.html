Jharkhand Vehicle VIP Number Plate Auction 2026: Online Registration, Fees & Process

Published: 2026-08-13

Jharkhand Transport Department car aur bike owners ko apne pasand ka VIP ya Fancy registration number lene ki facility deta hai. Agar aap apne naye vehicle ke liye 0001, 0007, 0786, 1111, ya 9999 jaisa special number chahte hai, toh aap Parivahan portal ke through online e-Auction me participate kar sakte hai.

Aapko RTO office ke chakkar lagane ki zaroorat nahi hai. Pura process Parivahan Fancy Number Booking Portal (fancy.parivahan.gov.in) par online hota hai. Is detailed guide me hum aapko Jharkhand Vehicle VIP Number Plate Auction ki category-wise price list, e-auction bidding rules, required documents, aur step-by-step registration process ke baare me bataenge.

📋 Step-by-Step Online Booking Steps

  1. Register Account: Visit fancy.parivahan.gov.in and sign up as a New Public User.
  2. Check Availability: Select your Jharkhand RTO (e.g., JH01) and search for desired 4-digit numbers.
  3. Pay Base Fee: Reserve the number by paying the base registration fee via online payment options.
  4. e-Auction / Allotment: Participate in bidding if multiple users selected the same number, or get an immediate Allotment Letter if uncontested.

Eligibility & Documents Required for VIP Number Plate:

Eligibility Criteria

  • Applicant Indian Citizen hona chahiye.
  • Vehicle Jharkhand RTO (jaise JH-01 Ranchi, JH-02 Hazaribagh, JH-05 Jamshedpur) me registered hona chahiye.
  • New vehicle purchase certificate ya booking invoice hona zaroori hai.

Important Documents

  • Identity Proof: Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, ya Voter ID Card.
  • Address Proof: Jharkhand state ka valid Address Proof (Aadhaar / Electricity Bill / Passport).
  • Vehicle Sale Certificate: Form 21, Form 22, aur Dealer Invoice copy.
  • Active Mobile Number & Email ID: OTP aur e-Auction notifications ke liye.

Step-by-Step Process to Apply for VIP Number Plate Online

  1. Official Parivahan Fancy portal (fancy.parivahan.gov.in) par jayein.
  2. Home page par New Public User option par click karke apna account banayein.
  3. Account login karne ke baad Jharkhand state aur apna RTO Office select karein.
  4. Search by Number option se dekhein ki aapka pasandida VIP number available hai ya nahi.
  5. Apne chosen number ko select karke Registration Fee (Non-Refundable) aur Reserve Price (EMD Amount) online pay karein.
  6. Online Payment hone ke baad aapko Unique Acknowledgment Number (UAN) milega.
  7. Scheduled bidding dates par online e-Auction window me login karein aur apna bid place karein.
  8. Bidding Jeetne ke baad bacha hua balance amount (Final Bid Amount minus Reserve Fee) online pay karein.
  9. System se Allotment Letter download aur print karein.
  10. Is Allotment Letter ko 90 dino ke andar apne RTO / Vehicle Dealer ko submit karke HSRP number plate lagwayein.

Rules for Auction Refund & Cancellation

  • If You Lose the Auction: Agar aap bidding me nahi jeet te hain, toh aapka jama kiya hua Reserve Amount (EMD) 7 se 10 working days ke andar aapke original bank account me refund ho jata hai.
  • If Single Applicant: Agar kisi VIP number ke liye sirf ek hi applicant hai, toh bina auction ke Base Reserve Price par hi number allot kar diya jata hai.
  • Non-Refundable Fee: Registration fee (normally ₹1,000) kisi bhi condition me refund nahi hoti.
  • Validity Period: VIP number allot hone ke 90 dino ke andar vehicle registration complete karna compulsory hai, varna allotment cancel ho jayega.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Jharkhand me 0001 number plate ki minimum price kitni hai?

Jharkhand me 0001 VIP number ka base reserve price ₹3 Lakh se ₹5 Lakh tak start hota hai. Final price auction ki bidding par depend karta hai.

2. Kya hum purani car ke liye VIP number plate le sakte hain?

Haan, agar aap ownership transfer kar rahe hain ya RTO re-registration karva rahe hain, toh aap fancy number book kar sakte hain.

3. e-Auction me haar jaane par paisa vapas milta hai ya nahi?

Haan, auction me bid nahi jeetne par aapka Base Reserve Fee (EMD) pure tarike se bank account me refund ho jata hai.

4. Allotment letter milne ke baad number plate lagwane ke liye kitna time milta hai?

Allotment letter issue hone ki date se 90 dino ke andar aapko apne RTO me vehicle register karwana hota hai.
