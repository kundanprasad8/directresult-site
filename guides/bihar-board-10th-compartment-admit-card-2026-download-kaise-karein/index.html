Bihar Board 10th Compartment Admit Card 2026: Matric Supplementary Exam Ka Admit Card Kaise Download Karein?

Published: 2026-06-08

Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB) ne recently Matric (Class 10th) Compartmental-cum-Special Examination 2026 ke liye online application process ko poora kar liya hai. Jo bache annual board result mein ek ya do subjects mein fail ho gaye the, unhone apna saal bachane ke liye is form ko bhara hai. Form bharne ke baad ab sabhi bacho ko sirf ek hi cheez ka intezar hai – unka Compartment Admit Card.

Bina admit card ke aapko exam hall mein entry nahi milegi, aur isme aapka roll number, exam center aur sittings ki sahi timings likhi hoti hain. Bahut baar bache internet par galat links par click karke apna time waste karte hain kyunki board ke rules ke according bache khud se ise direct download nahi kar sakte. Aaj is article mein, main aapko bseb 10th compartment admit card 2026 download kaise kare aur ise receive karne ka bilkul sahi aur official tarika batane wala hoon.

BSEB Matric Compartment Exam 2026: Important Dates

Bihar Board aamtaur par compartmental exams May ke mahine mein hi conduct karwata hai taaki result jald se jald jari karke bacho ka aage Class 11th (Inter) mein admission ka saal kharab na ho.

  • Admit Card Release Date: May 2026 ke teesre ya aakhri hafte mein portal par admit card live kiya jata hai.
  • BSEB 10th Compartment Exam Dates: Exam May ke aakhri hafte se lekar June ke pehle hafte tak alag-alag sittings mein conduct ki jayegi.
  • Practical Exam Dates: Agar aap kisi subject ke practical mein fail the, toh uski pariksha bhi theory se pehle ya baad mein aapke apne school center par hi li jayegi.

Important Rule: Kya Students Khud Se Admit Card Download Kar Sakte Hain?

Sabhie Students Ke Liye Zaroori Suchna: Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB) ke rules ke according, Matric Compartment exam ka original admit card bache khud se direct download nahi kar sakte.

Board ise sirf School Principal ke official login user ID aur password par jari karta hai. Isliye internet par chal rahe kisi bhi fake “Direct Download Link” par apni details daal kar apna samay kharab na karein.

School Ke Zariye Admit Card Kaise Milega?

Agar aapne compartmental exam ka form bhara hai, toh aapko apna admit card lene ke liye niche diye gaye steps ko follow karna hoga:

  1. Sabse pehle apne us school mein jayein jahan se aapne Class 10th ki padhai ki hai.
  2. Apne school ke examination clerk ya Principal office mein contact karein.
  3. School ke principal board ke portal se aapka admit card download karke, us par apna Official Seal aur Signature lagayenge.
  4. Yaad rakhein, bina principal ke signature aur school ke mohar ke aapka admit card exam center par valid nahi mana jayega. Isliye school se lete waqt ise zaroor check kar lein.

School Wale Portal Se Admit Card Kaise Download Karein? (For School Authorities)

Agar aap ek school principal hain ya administrator hain, toh aap niche diye gaye process se apne school ke sabhi bacho ka admit card ek sath download kar sakte hain:

1.Official Website Open Karein:

Sabse pehle Bihar Board ki official website secondary.biharboardonline.com par jayein.

2.Compartmental Portal Par Click Karein:

Homepage par aapko “Compartmental-cum-Special Exam 2026 Admit Card” ka link dikhega, us par click karein.

3.School Login Details Enter Karein:

Ab khule naye page par school ka unique User ID aur Password daal kar captcha code fill karein aur ‘Login’ button par click karein.

4.View/Print Admit Card:

Dashboard open hone ke baad “View/Print Admit Card” ke option par jayein. Yahan aap pure school ke bacho ki list ya kisi ek student ka Roll Code daal kar search kar sakte hain.

5.Bulk Download Aur Print:

‘Download All’ par click karke PDF file ko safe karein aur sabhi admit cards ka printout nikal kar bacho ko distribute karne ke liye taiyar karein.

Admit Card Milne Ke Baad In Cheezon Ko Zaroor Check Karein

Jaise hi aapko school se admit card mile, shaant dimag se niche di gayi details ko zaroor re-check kar lein:

  • Aapka Naam aur Spelling: Aapke aur aapke mata-pita ke naam ki spelling sahi honi chahiye.
  • Subject Codes: Check karein ki aapne jis subject ka compartment form bhara tha, sirf usi subject ka naam aur code wahan printed hai ya nahi.
  • Exam Center Name: Aapka exam center aapke purane main board exam wale center se alag ho sakta hai, isliye center ka naam aur address dhyan se padh lein.
  • Exam Date & Sitting: Kaun sa paper kis din hai aur kaun si sitting (First Sitting ya Second Sitting) mein hai, use note kar lein.

FAQs:

1. BSEB 10th compartment admit card 2026 check karne ki official website kya hai?

Iske liye official portal secondary.biharboardonline.com hai. School authorities isi website se login karke download karte hain.

2. Kya mujhe compartment exam ke liye naya roll number milega?

Haan, compartmental-cum-special exam ke liye Bihar Board ek naya dummy aur final admit card jari karta hai, jisme aapka roll number aur exam center dono naye ho sakte hain.

3. Agar mere admit card mein koi galti ho toh kya karein?

Agar photo galat ho ya subject galat ho, toh turant apne school ke principal se milein. School authority board ko application bhejkar ise exam se pehle theek karwa sakti hai.

4. Kya compartment admit card ke liye koi alag se fees deni hoti hai?

Nahi, jab aapne compartment ka form bhara tha, toh admit card ki fee usi mein shamil (include) thi. School se admit card lete waqt aapko koi extra paise nahi dene hain.

5. Agar main admit card ghar par bhool jau toh kya exam dene milega?

Bilkul nahi, bina valid original admit card aur ek identity proof (jaise Aadhar card) ke aapko exam hall mein baithne ki permission nahi di jayegi.
