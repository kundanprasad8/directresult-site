Major Festivals (Dates Vary by Lunar Calendar)

  • Holi — usually February/March (2 days in many states).
  • Eid-ul-Fitr / Eid-ul-Zuha — dates shift ~10 days earlier each year; the state list confirms them.
  • Diwali / Chhath — usually October/November; Chhath is a major holiday in Bihar, Jharkhand and eastern UP.
  • Durga Puja / Dussehra — usually September/October; the longest break in West Bengal.
  • State foundation day — Bihar observes its own statehood day as a public holiday.

Frequently Asked Questions

When do summer vacations start in Bihar schools?

Most schools in Bihar break for summer in mid-May to mid-June, though hill regions shift earlier and the exact dates come from the state education department's annual holiday notification each year.

When are winter vacations in Bihar?

Winter breaks typically run from the last week of December to the first week of January, longer in colder districts. Private schools may add a few days around it.

Do all schools in the state follow the same holiday list?

Government schools follow the state education department's list. Private and CBSE/ICSE schools follow most of it but add their own founder's days and local festivals — always confirm with your school diary.

Where is the official school holiday list published?

The Bihar education department publishes the yearly holiday calendar on its official website, usually in December for the next year. This page tracks the fixed national holidays plus the typical state pattern.

Is there a holiday on election or census days?

Schools used as polling centres or exam centres close on those days with a short-notice circular — these never appear in the yearly list, so watch school notices.
